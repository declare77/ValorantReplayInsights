Calibrate map fit (do this once per map, then forget about the sliders)

Instead of guessing slider values, click a few spots on the map where you can positively identify a player's real location (a doorway, a plant spot, a corner you recognize) and this works out the correct fit itself. It's saved per map, so once it's right here it'll be right for every future replay on the same map too — no need to redo it.

  1. Pause at a moment where you know exactly where a player is standing.
  2. Pick that player below, then click Pick location and click that spot on the map.
  3. Repeat for at least 4 points (6 or more is better) — spread out across different areas of the map, not clustered together — then click Compute & save fit. Fewer than 4 can't catch a mis-click: with exactly 3 the fit passes through all of them perfectly even if one is wrong, so there'd be no way to tell from the result alone.
